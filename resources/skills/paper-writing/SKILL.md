# Paper Writing

Turn verified research into a clear, evidence-aware manuscript. Use `$academic-research` for missing literature and `$research-report` when external claims need a source matrix. Use `$docx`, `$markdown`, or another format Skill only after the manuscript content is checked.

## Workflow

1. Confirm the target audience, paper type, research question, contribution, language, citation style, and requested output format.
2. Build an outline appropriate to the paper. For empirical work, use an IMRaD structure: introduction, related work, methods, results, discussion, limitations, and conclusion. Adapt it when the field requires a different structure.
3. Establish the argument chain: problem, gap, approach, evidence, result, implication. Every major claim must be supported by supplied evidence or a direct verified source.
4. Keep results, interpretation, hypotheses, and recommendations separate. Never turn a proposed result into a reported result.
5. Plan figures and tables before filling prose. Each should have a purpose, data provenance, caption, and reference from the text.
6. Run a citation and integrity pass: resolve duplicate references, flag missing metadata, check that citations support the sentence, and mark uncertain passages for author review.
7. Run a structure and clarity pass only after scientific correctness is stable.

## Integrity Rules

- Never invent data, sample sizes, metrics, statistical significance, references, DOI values, quotations, or reviewer decisions.
- Preserve the author's intended contribution and flag a possible change in claim scope instead of silently changing it.
- Mark placeholders such as `[需要补充实验结果]` clearly and do not hide them in polished prose.
- Do not guarantee acceptance, novelty, or compliance with a journal unless the relevant policy was checked.

## Default Output

1. Contribution and argument map
2. Paper outline
3. Draft or revised manuscript
4. Figure and table plan
5. Citation and evidence issues
6. Author decisions and missing materials
7. Final submission checklist
